Privacy Policy

Bayleaf Technologies · Suffolk County, New York · Effective November 5, 2026

Bayleaf Technologies ("Bayleaf," "we," "us") builds automated lead-response systems for small service businesses. This policy explains what information we collect when you interact with a form or phone number operated by Bayleaf on behalf of a participating business, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, mobile phone number, address or town, and a description of the work you are requesting, when you submit a web form or call a participating business.
  • Consent records: the date and time you checked the SMS consent box on the form, or placed a call to a number that states you will receive a text back.
  • Message records: the content and timestamps of text messages sent to you and any replies you send, including STOP or HELP requests.

How we use it

  • To send you a text message responding to your request, on behalf of the business you contacted.
  • To create or update your record in that business's customer relationship system so they can follow up.
  • To honor opt-out requests and keep records required by carrier regulations.

SMS messaging

Messages are conversational replies to your own request. Message frequency varies with your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for assistance.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who sees your information

Your information is shared only with the participating business you contacted and with the service providers that operate the system on our behalf (message delivery, form hosting, workflow automation, and CRM storage). These providers process data under contract and may not use it for their own purposes.

Retention

We keep records for as long as the participating business has an active relationship with you, and consent and opt-out records for as long as carrier regulations require.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out of further texts.
  • Email us at the address below to request a copy of your information or to have it deleted.
