SMS Opt-In Form

Bayleaf Technologies operates lead-intake forms on behalf of participating small service businesses. Customers consent to receive text messages by checking a required consent checkbox before submitting. The form cannot be submitted without it.

Live form: https://bayleaftech.app.n8n.cloud/form/demo-lead-form

Consent checkbox text

By submitting, you agree to receive a text reply from Harbor Roofing about your request. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

What the customer sees

After submission

On-screen confirmation: "Got it. Check your phone, we're texting you now." The customer then receives one text message responding to their request. Every message ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
